Executive Summary

What the store sells on day one, and why.

The launch catalog contains 15 prompt packs across 5 job categories, 5 practical ebooks (36–52 pages), 3 bundles, and 2 lead magnets — 25 sellable products plus a free funnel. Structure follows the Step 1 research: niche over volume, tiered price points per category, ebooks positioned as the premium "method" products (priced above packs, per catalog spec), and bundles at a visible ~27% discount. Every product ships quality-checked (Word + PDF for packs, PDF + EPUB for ebooks), and the SKU system is built to scale across future categories.

Pricing Philosophy

How Step 1 research principles become prices.

Research principle (Step 1)How it shows up in this catalog
Niche beats volumePacks are 12–45 prompts per single job task — never "10,000 mega packs."
Tiered options per categoryStarter packs (12–15 prompts) → System packs (35–45 prompts) → Ebook (premium method).
Ebooks priced higher than packsPer catalog spec, ebooks carry the full method + workbook and are the premium product in each category (GH₵ 165–245 vs packs GH₵ 75–225).
Below-cheap signals garbageNo product below GH₵ 75 (~$6.7); free lead magnets are clearly "samples," not products.
Bundles at visible discount~26–27% off combined value on every bundle.
Regional pricingLocal-currency prices set for Ghanaian purchasing power; USD storefront in Wave 3.
Why ebooks cost more than packs
A prompt pack is a tool (copy-paste, 15–45 items); an ebook is a system (method + walkthroughs + checklists + the prompts embedded). The spec requires ebook > pack, and this is defensible: ebooks remain far below the $17–43 Kindle price-fatigue zone documented in Step 1, and every ebook ships with a free first chapter to de-risk purchase.

Quality Standard Checklists

Every product passes its checklist before it appears in the store.

📦 Prompt pack quality standards

  • Context instructions — every prompt includes explicit role, goal, audience, tone, and output constraints; no bare instructions.
  • Variable placeholders — inputs in [BRACKETED] placeholders with one-line fill-in instructions.
  • Example outputs — each prompt ships with at least one annotated sample output.
  • Consistent formatting — uniform template: Title · When to use · The prompt · How to customize · Example output; monospace prompt blocks; one prompt per page.
  • Anti-generic constraints — banned-phrase lists and output-format specs kill the "AI tell."
  • Testing — every prompt tested on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini; limitations documented in a README.
  • Value test — passes Step 1's rule: "the user cannot get 80% of this value by asking the AI directly."
  • Files — Word (.docx) + PDF, no watermarks, print-safe, with a copy-paste plain-text section.
  • Updates — changelog + 12 months of free prompt updates by email.

📖 Ebook quality standards

  • Clear table of contents — chapter list with page numbers; hyperlinked in the EPUB.
  • Cover page — designed cover, title page, copyright/disclaimer page, author/about page.
  • Professional formatting — consistent H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, no orphaned headings, consistent spacing, page numbers.
  • Readable fonts — body ≥ 11pt, 1.5 line spacing, comfortable margins; EPUB tested on Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books.
  • Consistent headings — every chapter opens with number + title; one section style throughout.
  • Actionable content on every page — each page carries at least one takeaway, template, prompt, or checklist; zero filler pages.
  • Chapter structure — 5–7 chapters per book; every chapter ends with "Action Steps."
  • Editing pass — two-pass human proofread; zero typos; AI-tell-free writing.
  • Formats — PDF (6×9 print-ready) + EPUB (reflowable, DRM-free).
  • Sample — Chapter 1 free on the product page.

Future Catalog Expansion Roadmap

How the catalog scales after launch — new categories, formats, subscriptions, and markets.

Wave 1 — Launch · Weeks 0–4 · this catalog

  • 15 prompt packs · 5 ebooks · 3 bundles · 2 lead magnets · storefront with instant delivery, PDF+EPUB, re-download page, human support, 14-day refund, free update emails.

Wave 2 — New categories & formats · Weeks 5–10

  • HR/Recruiters category (highest-evidence runner-up from Step 1): 2 starter packs (JD writer; interview & screening) + 1 system pack + ebook The Recruiter's AI Desk (40 pp).
  • Teachers category: 2 packs (lesson plans & rubrics; parent emails & admin) — Step 1 showed teachers already pay $19 for role packs.
  • Notion-ready versions of the top 3 packs (CSV import + Notion template) — answers the "how do I organize prompts" pain.
  • BND-SMB-01 Small Business Bundle and BND-DEV-01 Dev Bundle from existing inventory (no new content needed).
  • Paystack / MoMo checkout integration if not already at launch.

Wave 3 — Depth & subscription · Months 3–4

  • Lawyers category: 2 packs focused on safe drafting workflows (hallucination-avoidance) — high caution, high willingness to pay.
  • Second ebooks per category: Freelance Pricing Without Guessing (FRE), The Content Calendar That Runs Itself (MKT), Analytics for Creators (CRE), SQL Interview Prep (DEV).
  • All-Access Launch Bundle: everything at ~30% off (value GH₵ 2,710 → ~GH₵ 1,899).
  • Prompt Updates Club subscription: GH₵ 99/month — quarterly prompt refreshes + 2 new packs/year (defuses staleness, builds recurring revenue).
  • International storefront: USD pricing tier using research benchmarks ($9–19 starter packs, $19–29 systems, $24.99–39.99 bundles).

Wave 4 — Scale · Months 5+

  • Courses: Prompt Mastery for African Professionals (GH₵ 499) built from the top ebook content.
  • Custom prompt service: done-for-you packs from GH₵ 549 (high-margin, no inventory).
  • Community: paid membership (GH₵ 79/month) with monthly prompt drops + live Q&A.
  • Localization: French (francophone West Africa) and Swahili editions of the top 3 ebooks; regional currency support (NGN, XOF).
  • Print-on-demand versions of the top 2 ebooks for the local market.
