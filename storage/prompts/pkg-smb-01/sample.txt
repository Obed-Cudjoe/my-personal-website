### Google review reply - negative
Write a reply to this negative review: '[REVIEW TEXT]'. Rules: (1) apologize for the specific experience without grovelling, (2) state the facts calmly if the review is inaccurate, (3) offer a concrete fix offline (phone/visit), (4) never argue or mention the reviewer's tone. Under 80 words. End with an invitation to give us another chance.

### Review request to happy customers
Write a short message asking [CUSTOMER] for a Google review after [WHAT THEY BOUGHT]. Make it easy: (1) one line on why reviews help a small business like ours, (2) a direct link, (3) a one-question prompt: 'What would you tell a friend about us?'. Optionally offer a small thank-you ([INCENTIVE]). Under 60 words, text-message friendly.
