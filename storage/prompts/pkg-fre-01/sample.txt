Freelance Client Machine: Outreach & Proposals

### Cold outreach that gets replies
Write a cold outreach message from [ME - freelance role] to [PROSPECT NAME] at [COMPANY] who [WHY THEY MATTER - e.g. just hired, wrote a post, expanded]. Goal: book a 15-minute call. Lead with one specific observation about their business or a post they wrote — not about me. Keep under 120 words. End with one low-friction question. Forbidden: 'I hope this finds you well', 'I wanted to reach out', 'passionate about'.

### Proposal that sells the outcome
Write a project proposal for [CLIENT] for [PROJECT]. Client's goal: [THEIR GOAL]. My approach: [YOUR APPROACH - 2-3 sentences]. Timeline: [X] weeks. Budget: GH₵ [AMOUNT]. Structure: (1) the outcome they care about, (2) what I will deliver week by week, (3) what I need from them, (4) price with 2 options - full scope and a lean version. Tone: confident, plain language, no jargon. Under 400 words.

### Discovery call opener
I have a 20-minute discovery call with [PROSPECT] at [COMPANY] about [TOPIC]. Write me: (1) a 30-second opener that names the pain they likely have, (2) 8 discovery questions that move from their situation to their goals to budget, (3) a polite way to ask 'what happens if we do nothing'. Keep questions open-ended, no yes/no.
