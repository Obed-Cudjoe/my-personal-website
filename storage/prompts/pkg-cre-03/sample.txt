Creator Complete System

### Idea mining from your niche
Mine 15 video ideas for [NICHE] targeting [AUDIENCE] from: (1) the top 20 videos in the niche (what angles repeat, what's missing), (2) questions people ask in comments, (3) my own expertise [MY SKILLS]. Label each idea: evergreen or trending, and the hook angle. No 'reacts' or 'top 10' filler unless it fits the channel.

### Keyword research for creators
For the topic '[TOPIC]', research the angle space: (1) 8 search phrases people use (long-tail), (2) which existing videos rank and what they cover, (3) 3 angles not covered, (4) a recommendation: which angle I should make first and why. Act as a YouTube strategist.

### Viewer avatar
Build a viewer avatar for [CHANNEL NICHE]: demographics, what they watch before bed, their level of knowledge, their 3 biggest frustrations, the words they search, what makes them click vs scroll, what makes them subscribe vs just watch. Then write 3 content briefs this avatar would click.
